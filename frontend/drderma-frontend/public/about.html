About DrDerma AI

DrDerma is an intelligent AI-based skin diagnosis assistant built to bridge the gap between initial symptom detection and professional medical consultation. It uses advanced image embeddings and similarity models to compare user-uploaded images with known disease patterns.

What makes DrDerma unique is its adaptive questioning system. Instead of relying only on visual data, it asks relevant follow-up questions that refine the prediction process. This significantly increases diagnostic confidence.

The system evaluates multiple possible conditions, assigns scores based on both image similarity and user responses, and gradually narrows down to the most probable diagnosis.

DrDerma is not a replacement for professional medical advice, but it provides a fast, accessible, and intelligent first step toward understanding skin conditions.
